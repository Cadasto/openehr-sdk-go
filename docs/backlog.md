Lines are grouped by theme so one delivery can take a whole group; the sentence under each heading says what such a delivery is. A delivery whose `Files` touch a line's path verifies it, and fixes it or finds it stale; the next `/sdd-triage --backlog` deletes what is settled. `sdd-pr harvest` and `sdd-pr flip --carry` add new lines under `## Unsorted`, defects first; the next `/sdd-triage --backlog` sorts them into a group.
