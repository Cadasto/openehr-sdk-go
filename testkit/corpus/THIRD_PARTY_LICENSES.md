**REQ-116 sibling-naming oracles (`webtemplate/`):** `Corona_Anamnese.opt` + `Corona_Anamnese.webtemplate.json` (upstream `corona_anamnese.opt` / `webtemplate/corona_anamnese.json`) and `GECCO_Diagnose.opt` + `GECCO_Diagnose.webtemplate.json` (upstream `GECCO_Diagnose.opt` / `webtemplate/GECCO_Diagnose.json`), pinned at the same commit `e57511c6aca27ed501d31d663762c37c3491e74e`. Vendored unmodified as the REQ-116 oracles ([PR 84](https://github.com/Cadasto/openehr-sdk-go/pull/84)) covering the gap's two failure modes: `Corona_Anamnese` failed loudly (four `SECTION.adhoc.v1` siblings reuse one archetype, and eight reused screening OBSERVATIONs under Symptome do the same — `webtemplate.Build` returned `ErrIDCollision`; its golden carries 350 name-predicate segments over 213 `aqlPath`s), `GECCO_Diagnose` diverged silently (it built, but 24 reference `aqlPath`s carry 30 name-predicate segments the builder did not emit). REQ-116 closed both, and the pair now serves as the PROBE-075 parity oracles at 230/230 and 34/34. Not part of the SDK runtime.
